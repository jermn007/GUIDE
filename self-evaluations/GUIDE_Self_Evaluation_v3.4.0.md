## What changed for the document

1. **Visual model added.** The three-alignments diagram is the first figure in the framework's history. Every prior self-evaluation flagged its absence as the reason Archetype 04's Multimedia Principle Compliance was stuck at 3.
2. **Dual-mode and Design targets ported in.** The document previously described evaluation only; it now leads with both modes and gives each archetype a short forward-looking Design targets list.

## Key finding: the visual-model gap finally closes

Across v2.2.0, v3.0.0, v3.1.1, v3.2.3, and v3.3.0, one recommendation recurred: **add a visual model** (Merrill's three quality outcomes / the alignment triangle). Its absence held **Archetype 04, Multimedia Principle Compliance, at 3** - the framework taught multimedia principles it did not itself follow.

v3.4.0 resolves it. The updated document contains the three-alignments diagram, so it now practices the multimedia design it preaches. **Multimedia Principle Compliance moves 3 -> 4** (a 5 would require figures throughout, not one; this is one strong, load-appropriate diagram). The same iconic visual gives Archetype 09's Emotional Engagement a mental model to attach to (**3 -> 4**).
