# GUIDE Framework - Self-Evaluation v3.5.0

## The framework as shipped, scored against the rubrics as they are written today

**Date:** November 2026
**Method:** Each applicable archetype's scoring anchors, read in full from the current `archetypes/handoff_NN_*.md`, applied to the artifact. Every dimension was re-scored from evidence; no v3.4.0 score was carried forward without checking that its evidence still holds.
**Purpose:** Re-score after the v3.5.0 skill restructure (`EVALUATE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, the skill-edition rubrics, three typed commands) and the Assessment Design (02) rubric edit - and test whether the earlier scores survive a strict reading of the anchors.

---

## A note on the artifact being evaluated

Earlier self-evaluations scored one surface at a time: v3.1.1 through v3.3.0 scored the **living framework** (handoffs, skill, README); v3.0.0 and v3.4.0 scored the **printable `GUIDE_Rubric_Document.docx`**.

Both ship in v3.5.0, so this evaluation scores **both together** - the framework as a user receives it:

- `archetypes/handoff_NN_*.md` - the ten canonical rubrics (single source of truth)
- `skill/guide-instructional-design/` - `SKILL.md` (router), `EVALUATE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `references/00_archetype_index.md`, and the skill-edition handoffs, plus the identical plugin mirror
- the three plugin commands: `guide-review`, `guide-design`, `guide-pipeline`
- `README.md`, `STATUS.md`, the plugin `README.md`
- `GUIDE_Rubric_Document.docx` (stamped 3.5.0)

One rule follows from scoring two surfaces: **where the living layer and the docx differ, the weaker one sets the score** for dimensions about accuracy, consistency, and accessibility. A reader who holds only the docx, or only the skill, gets what that surface says.

Conventions are unchanged from prior self-evaluations: six archetypes apply to GUIDE itself (01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 09); archetype 10 is scored and reported separately; 06, 07, and 08 score artifact types GUIDE is not and are N/A.

This evaluation ran in two passes. The first pass found defects that were then fixed before release (listed below). The scores here describe the repository after those fixes, each one re-read in the file rather than taken on report.

## What changed v3.4.0 -> v3.5.0

1. **Skill restructure.** `SKILL.md` is now a short router. It sends each run to `EVALUATE.md` or `DESIGN.md`, and each of those ends in a checkable "Done when" list. The skill's `references/handoff_*.md` are a slimmer skill edition generated by `skill/build_skill.py` (judge prompts and harness notes dropped, about a fifth smaller). The canonical handoffs stay complete.
2. **Three typed commands** in the plugin (`/guide-review`, `/guide-design`, `/guide-pipeline`), and Commands and "It's working if" sections in the plugin README.
3. **Rubric 02 edited.** Bloom's Alignment anchors now score the match between each item and its objective, not how high the items reach. New guidance covers criterion- versus norm-referenced tests, scoring a single item or short sample, and what to do when objective quality and item mapping disagree. A "Missing or Wrong Answer Key" severity flag was added, with a cap of 2 on Reliability Considerations when a complete assessment has no key.
4. **Small anchor clarifications** moved from judge prompts into kept sections of handoffs 01, 03, 06, 08, 09, and 10 (01's overall weighting, 03's "events may be reordered", and similar).
5. **The docx** received the new 02 Bloom's anchors, corrected archetype and dimension counts, and the 3.5.0 stamp. It does not carry the new 02 guidance on samples, test type, or answer keys.

### Fixed between the first and second pass

These were found by the first pass and are confirmed fixed in the files:

- `EVALUATE.md` no longer points to a troubleshooting table in handoff 01 that did not exist; it now names the three handoffs (02, 04, 10) that have one.
- The agent lists in `README.md` and the plugin README now add up to 13 (the curriculum-alignment specialist is one of the ten and doubles as the gate).
- The docx no longer says "nine archetypes" or "54 dimensions" in its Executive Summary, Architecture, or Implementation Guide.
- Handoff 09 dates Dolcos, LaBar & Cabeza to 2004.
- Handoff 02's worked example and its Bloom's improvement suggestion follow the match reading, and the docx's Bloom's foundation sentence agrees with the anchors beneath it.
- `STATUS.md` now marks "Printable rubric matches the live framework" as `open` and lists the known drift.

## Reading this score against the history table

The composite is **4.1**, down from 4.4. Most of that fall is a re-baseline, not a decline in the framework. A reader of the history table should separate two things.

**1. Moves caused by v3.5.0 (two dimensions, -1 each).** The 02 rubric edit added rules to the live rubric that the docx does not have, so the two shipped editions now score some artifacts differently (02 Reliability Considerations, 4 -> 3). And the new 02 rules and the three commands shipped without an edge case or a live run behind them (10 Coverage Completeness, 5 -> 4).

**2. Corrections to earlier, more generous readings (eight dimensions).** These scores were carried forward from release to release as deltas. Read against the anchor text and a specific file, they do not hold. The defects are older than v3.5.0: missing alt text and table headers in the docx, source names no rubric cites, a 5 for transfer and retention with no spaced review anywhere, and so on. v3.5.0 did not create them. This evaluation is the first to look.

The arithmetic, on the six applicable archetypes (36 dimension scores):

| | Points | Composite |
|---|---|---|
| v3.4.0 as published | 157 / 36 | 4.36 |
| Eight corrections (-9 points) | 148 / 36 | 4.11 |
| One v3.5.0-caused move (-1 point) | 147 / 36 | **4.08** |

So about 0.25 of the 0.28 fall is correction, and about 0.03 is this release. The 4.11 row is indicative only: it applies today's corrections to the v3.4.0 scores and is not a re-score of the v3.4.0 artifact.

No dimension moved up. The v3.5.0 improvements are real, but they landed on dimensions already at 5 (04 Extraneous Load, 04 Intrinsic Load, 03 Sequencing Logic), or on dimensions that cannot rise until the work is verified (04 Interactivity waits on a live command run), or they turned a score that had been held on a generous reading into one that is now earned (03 Gagne's Nine Events, 02 Bloom's Alignment).

By GUIDE's own threshold bands, 4.08 is "light revision; ship with documented gaps". This file is that documentation.

## SUMMARY

v3.5.0 restructures the skill around a short router and two mode procedures with checkable endings, adds three typed commands, and corrects a real flaw in the Assessment Design rubric, where a recall quiz for a recall objective could not score above 2.

Scored against the rubrics as they are written today, with both shipped surfaces in scope, the framework comes out at **4.1** on the six applicable archetypes and **4.1** with archetype 10, down from 4.4 and 4.3. Ten dimensions moved down and none moved up.
